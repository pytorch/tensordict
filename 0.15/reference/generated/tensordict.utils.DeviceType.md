# DeviceType

tensordict.utils.DeviceType

The type of the `device` arguments.

A [`torch.device`](https://docs.pytorch.org/docs/stable/tensor_attributes.html#torch.device), a str such as `"cpu"` or `"cuda:0"`, or the index
of a CUDA device.

alias of [`Union`](https://docs.python.org/3/library/typing.html#typing.Union)[[`device`](https://docs.pytorch.org/docs/stable/tensor_attributes.html#torch.device), [`str`](https://docs.python.org/3/builtins/stdtypes.html#str), [`int`](https://docs.python.org/3/builtins/functions.html#int)]
